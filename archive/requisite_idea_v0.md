# Current idea (raw, v0, 2026-10-23)

**The team's idea:** an agent system for supply chain and procurement.
1. You tell it what part you want, plus parameters (quantity, specs, deadline, budget, etc.).
2. It finds the best supplier within those parameters.
3. It runs the purchase through to the books: PO, receipt, invoice, payment, journal entries, close.
4. **It learns the rules your company works by.** Hyphenate emphasized this point to the team at their booth.

**What Hyphenate told the team:** they want the project to be **heavily focused on automating accounting**. They did not like a niche winery idea.

**Team background:** robotics, satellites and hardware. Buying physical parts (components, assemblies) is the natural setting.
