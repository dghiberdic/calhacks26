Task: draft the written notice that Article 14(2) of Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 requires for a cancelled flight. It sets out the rules for compensation and assistance that apply to this passenger's entitlement and gives the contact details of the national enforcement body.

One letter text goes to every passenger on the flight with this entitlement, so it contains no personal data. Use exactly these placeholders and no others:
- {{passenger_name}} in the greeting;
- {{booking_reference}} where you refer to the booking;
- {{neb_contact}} for the national enforcement body's contact details.
Never write a real name, email address, phone number or postal address.

Content:
- Name the flight and date from the evidence and say it was cancelled. Give the cause only in the words of the evidence's "cause"; when it is null, do not name a cause.
- Compensation, by status:
  - owed: state the amount exactly as given and how it is paid (Article 7(3): cash, bank transfer or cheque; travel vouchers only with the passenger's signed agreement);
  - no_compensation: include the exact phrase "no compensation; refunds and care still due" and the reason (extraordinary circumstances, Article 5(3));
  - any other status: say the entitlement is still being assessed and a decision will follow.
- Refund or re-routing (Article 8): if a refund was already initiated, say so and that it will not be repeated.
- Care (Article 9), when care is due.
- If <corrects> is present, say plainly near the top that an earlier message was wrong, quote what it got wrong, and give the correct position.
- Sign off as "Northstar Air Customer Care".

Reply with the letter text only.
